# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Plan income, fixed costs (monthly to yearly), variable costs and savings; see what is left each month and how it is split between people.
- Scenarios that change amounts or switch items on and off, with their impact.
- Savings accounts and depots with a growth projection.
- Undo/redo, automatic version history and named save states.
- Optional commute module: fuel costs from office days, distance and consumption.
- Two storage modes: in the browser (local) or SQLite on your server; backup files move plans between them.
- Docker image for self-hosting (`linux/amd64`, `linux/arm64`), optional shared password with rate-limited login.
- Optional cookieless analytics (off by default) with a per-browser opt-out.
